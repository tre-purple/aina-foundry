## Overview

The GoFarm Mālama ʻĀina Planner (MAP) is a production planning platform designed for Hawaiʻi's agricultural landscape. It moves farmers from manual spreadsheet tracking to a data-driven system that brings together CTAHR crop data, market pricing, and geospatial information.

The project is led by Mikey at GoFarm Hawaiʻi, the beginning-farmer training program, and was accepted into the University of Hawaiʻi's P2P (Product 2 Patent) incubator, which comes with funding. ʻĀina Foundry is working on a co-development plan with him.

A farmer's path through MAP follows the season: select a plot, build a production plan, lay out beds, follow the calendar, and keep field notes. Pick crops from a gallery pre-populated with days to maturity, spacing, and CTAHR research. Set a harvest window and the planting dates fill in. Drag crops onto bed layouts, sized from the plot's acreage, and see recommended bed feet or expected yield, whichever you start from. Plans can be exported to a spreadsheet and synced to a calendar.

ʻĀina Foundry's contribution builds on the same values as the rest of our kilo work. Beyond public reference data, GoFarmers record their own days to maturity, yields, and kilo, so over time the planner learns what works in their ʻāina, not just what the research says. AI is meant to support that practice, not replace it: pointing farmers toward the people who know the practice best, and encouraging them to trust their own kilo.

## Features

- **Production plans** — choose crops and a harvest window; planting and harvest dates are calculated from each crop's days to maturity
- **Crop cards** — a consistent detail page for every crop, with CTAHR research links
- **Bed planning** — drag crops onto bed layouts sized from plot acreage, with bed feet and yield calculated from each other, and successions shown in the same bed
- **CSA basket planning** — plan weekly baskets across the harvest window
- **Field notes** — dated notes linked to a production plan, with @-mentions for crops and topics like integrated pest management
- **Calendar and exports** — seeding, transplanting, and harvest dates on a calendar, with Google Calendar sync and spreadsheet export
- **Planned integrations** — FarmLink Hawaiʻi and USDA market pricing, Hawaiʻi climate and soil data, and local pest alerts

## Phase

Early planning. The product spec is written and architecture decisions are underway; there is no public codebase yet.
