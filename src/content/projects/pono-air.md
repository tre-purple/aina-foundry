## Overview

Communities near Kīlauea's lower East Rift Zone and in areas prone to wildfire smoke have limited access to real-time air quality data. The EPA monitoring network is sparse, with the nearest official stations often many miles away from affected neighborhoods.

Pono Air fills this gap with a community-operated sensor network using PurpleAir-class sensors, deployed on community buildings, schools, and trusted residents' homes.

## System Components

- **Sensor nodes** — PMS5003 + SGP30 sensors in weatherproof enclosures with WiFi or cellular uplink
- **Data aggregation** — Custom pipeline normalizing raw sensor data with EPA correction factors for Hawaiian volcanic aerosols (laze, vog)
- **Public dashboard** — Real-time and historical AQI maps, accessible at pono.air
- **SMS alerts** — Community members subscribe to receive alerts when their neighborhood exceeds unhealthy thresholds

## Health Impact Focus

Data from Pono Air has been used by local schools to make recess and outdoor activity decisions during high vog events. The project has also produced a community-authored health guide in Hawaiian and English for managing vog and wildfire smoke exposure.
