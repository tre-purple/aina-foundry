## Overview

Kalo — taro — is the cultural cornerstone of Hawaiian civilization. The diversification of kalo varieties, the health of loʻi systems, and the continuity of cultivation practices are central to cultural and food sovereignty for many Hawaiian families and communities.

Kalo AI is a mobile-first tool that gives farmers and stewards the ability to photograph kalo and receive immediate feedback on variety identification, health indicators, and recommended care — informed by both scientific literature and traditional ecological knowledge contributed by kalo farmers.

## Features

- **Variety identification** — trained on 80+ Hawaiian kalo varieties with input from expert practitioners
- **Disease detection** — recognizes common pathogens (Phytophthora, Pythium) and nutritional deficiencies before they become critical
- **Pest pressure indicators** — visual markers for slug damage, aphid colonies, and other common pests
- **Growth tracking** — photo time series to track individual plant health and loʻi productivity

## Cultural Grounding

The variety identification model was built with explicit consent from kalo farming families, with model weights and training data governed under a community data agreement. Families can withdraw their data and their varieties from the model at any time.

Traditional names, moʻolelo associated with each variety, and planting guidance from kūpuna are embedded in the variety profiles — ensuring the tool transmits cultural knowledge, not just agronomic data.

## Status

Prototype deployed with 3 loʻi sites. Currently expanding training dataset and conducting field accuracy evaluations.
