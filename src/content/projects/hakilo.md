## Overview

Kilo is the Hawaiian practice of deep observation: reading the sky, ocean, land, and living things to understand the world and our place in it, as our kūpuna did when choosing when to fish or plant. Hākilo is a mobile-first platform that digitizes that practice. It is live on the App Store.

Each day, users record a short kilo by voice and photo. The app organizes observations using Papakū Makawalu, which sorts the natural world into three houses: Papahulilani (sky and atmosphere), Papahulihonua (earth and water), and Papahānaumoku (living things). A fourth naʻau reflection asks how the person is doing internally. Every entry is tagged with the Kaulana Mahina moon phase, anahulu, and season, and every entry is private by default.

At MAʻO Farms, kilo connects haumāna to the ʻāina and to their own growth. The project also supports moʻolelo data practices, using storytelling rather than surveys alone to understand program impact.

We work alongside multiple external organizations and cultural practitioners to ensure the content we publish is true to our culture, and that we work alongside the community rather than as just a client. The organizations and practitioners we work with are MAʻO Farms, GoFarm Hawaii, Aunty Hiʻilani Shibata from Ka Mahina Project, and Kumu Kekuhi from Edith Kanakaʻole Foundation.

## Journey

The first Kilo Tracker prototype was built at the OYLN vibe coding workshop in August 2025. Between November 2025 and February 2026 it grew into Hākilo, and the kula team tested it in March 2026. The app ran as a summer pilot in MAʻO Farms' programs from June to August 2026. This fall, interns are continuing development.

## Features

- **Voice-first kilo entry** — record daily observations by voice, or type instead, with multiple photos
- **Papakū Makawalu daily kilo** — guided prompts for Papahulilani, Papahulihonua, and Papahānaumoku, plus a naʻau reflection
- **Kaulana Mahina** — moon phase, anahulu, and season added to every entry automatically, with moon phase reminders
- **Journal** — a moon phase calendar of past entries, with PDF export
- **Learn** — introductions to Papakū Makawalu, kilo, and Kaulana Mahina
- **Private by default** — entries are encrypted and stay with the person who wrote them

## Phase

Live on the App Store after a summer 2026 pilot with MAʻO Farms. Interns are continuing development this fall.
