## Overview

Kilo — the Hawaiian practice of observation — is central to how MAʻO Farms connects its haumāna (students) to the land and to their own growth. The Kilo Tracker puts that practice into a mobile app: each day, haumāna record a "weather ball" — a voice note and photos capturing what they observe and how they're doing.

Admins (program staff) see an aggregated, private view of haumāna data, giving them a window into participant wellbeing without requiring face-to-face check-ins for every student. Admins can also send custom prompts to haumāna after certain events, guiding reflection and engagement.

The app is being built as a native mobile experience using Expo (React Native), with the current build going through Apple TestFlight for iOS testing. The project also ties into broader work around moʻolelo data practices — using digital tools to support storytelling-based impact measurement rather than traditional data collection.

## Features

- **Voice + image kilo logging** — haumāna record daily observations with voice notes and multiple photos
- **Daily "weather ball"** — structured daily entry capturing personal and environmental observations
- **Admin aggregated view** — private dashboard showing haumāna data patterns without exposing individual entries broadly
- **Custom admin prompts** — staff can send targeted prompts to haumāna after specific events or milestones
- **Native mobile app** — built with Expo, currently in Apple TestFlight (v1.0.1)
