## Overview

AI 101 is Purple Maiʻa's introductory course on artificial intelligence, built "from the ground up" for people starting with no background. It was first presented at a Purple Maiʻa all-hands session and lives on as a self-paced learning hub that anyone can work through.

Learners move through a series of modules, with reference material alongside: every term from the modules explained in plain words, prompts to copy and adapt, answers to common questions, and links to videos, hands-on demos, research, and the slide deck. Progress is saved in the learner's own browser, and finishing the modules earns a certificate.

## Features

- **Modules** — a guided path through the fundamentals of AI
- **Glossary** — every term from the modules in plain language
- **Prompt library** — ready-to-use prompts with bracketed parts to swap for your own
- **FAQ and resources** — common questions answered, plus videos, demos, research, and slides
- **Completion certificate** — issued after finishing the modules
- **Private progress** — progress is saved in the learner's browser only

## Phase

Live and in use. Certificate issuing and UI improvements in progress.
